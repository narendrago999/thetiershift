AI Engineer

Build useful, responsible and production-ready AI experiences that help businesses work smarter.

About the opportunity

TierShift Technologies is looking for an AI Engineer who enjoys turning emerging AI capabilities into dependable products. You will help design, build and improve LLM-powered applications, retrieval systems, intelligent agents and integrations that solve real business problems.

AI EngineeringFull-timeRemote / Hybrid discussionIndia

What you'll do

  • Design and build applications powered by large language models (LLMs) and generative AI.
  • Develop retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflows, document processing and knowledge assistants.
  • Build AI agents and tool-calling workflows that integrate with business systems and APIs.
  • Develop backend services and AI integrations using Python and modern application frameworks.
  • Work with embeddings, vector databases, search, chunking and retrieval strategies.
  • Evaluate model quality, relevance, latency and cost; create repeatable testing and evaluation workflows.
  • Apply responsible AI, privacy, security, access control and guardrails to production solutions.
  • Deploy, monitor and optimise AI workloads on cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS or Google Cloud.
  • Collaborate with data, software and business teams to translate use cases into reliable solutions.

Required skills

  • Strong Python programming and practical software engineering fundamentals.
  • Hands-on understanding of LLMs, prompt design and generative AI application patterns.
  • Experience building or integrating APIs and backend services.
  • Understanding of RAG concepts, embeddings, semantic search and vector stores.
  • Familiarity with data handling, SQL, Git, testing and debugging.
  • Awareness of AI evaluation, hallucination mitigation, security and responsible AI practices.
  • Ability to communicate technical ideas clearly and work through ambiguous problems.

Good to have

  • Experience with LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel or similar frameworks.
  • Exposure to Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI.
  • Experience with vector databases such as Azure AI Search, pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate or FAISS.
  • Knowledge of Docker, CI/CD, observability and deploying scalable cloud applications.
  • Experience with agent orchestration, multi-agent patterns or enterprise knowledge systems.

What we value

  • Curiosity and a habit of learning in a fast-moving AI landscape.
  • Pragmatic judgement: choosing the right model and architecture for the problem.
  • Ownership, collaboration and a focus on measurable business outcomes.

Employment: Full-time. Work arrangement: Remote / Hybrid options will be discussed during the process. Compensation will be discussed based on experience, role scope and location.
