Editorial Policy

How TierShift Insights researches, reviews, updates and corrects its technology content.

This policy explains how TierShift Technologies prepares, reviews, updates and corrects material published in the Insights section.

Purpose and scope

TierShift Insights publishes educational articles and practical perspectives on data engineering, analytics, AI, cloud and software engineering. Content is intended for general information and should not be treated as legal, financial, security or professional advice for a specific organisation.

Research and sourcing

Writers should use reliable primary sources where possible, including official product documentation, standards and technical references. Articles should distinguish documented facts from recommendations, opinions and illustrative examples. Material claims should be supported by links or citations where appropriate.

Accuracy and technical review

Before publication, content should be checked for clarity, technical consistency, relevance and working links. Product interfaces, pricing, service limits and platform capabilities can change; readers should verify current details against the relevant vendor documentation before making implementation decisions.

Publication under the TierShift Technologies name does not mean an article has been independently certified or externally audited. Where an article receives a named technical review, the reviewer should be identified on that article.

Corrections and updates

We aim to correct material errors when they are brought to our attention. Significant updates should be reflected in the article's update date and, where useful, a short note explaining what changed. Readers can report an error by emailing narendrago999@gmail.com with the article URL and relevant details.

Independence and commercial disclosures

Editorial content should not be presented as an independent product review where a commercial relationship exists. Any paid placement, sponsorship, affiliate link or material commercial relationship will be clearly disclosed in or near the relevant content. At the time of this policy's update, no affiliate or sponsored content is being represented here.

Use of AI tools

AI tools may assist with research organisation, drafting or editing. Published content remains the responsibility of TierShift Technologies and should be checked for factual accuracy, originality, usefulness and appropriate sourcing before publication. AI-generated output is not treated as an authoritative source by itself.

Contact the editorial team

Send corrections or editorial questions to narendrago999@gmail.com.
