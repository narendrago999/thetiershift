Privacy Policy

How TierShift Technologies handles website enquiries, technical information, cookies, analytics and advertising.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TierShift Technologies ("TierShift", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit thetiershift.com, read our Insights articles or contact us.

This is a general website privacy notice. It should be reviewed and adapted to the actual tools, legal entity and jurisdictions applicable to the business before advertising or additional tracking technologies are activated.

1. Information you provide

If you contact us by email or use the website's enquiry interface, information you choose to provide may include your name, business email address, organisation, project details and any other information in your message. The current website enquiry interface prepares a message in your default email application; it does not submit the message to a website database. Your email provider will process the message when you send it.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, special-category personal data or confidential information unless we have specifically agreed a secure method for receiving it.

2. Information collected by hosting and technical services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a visitor requests a web page, the hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, date and time, and security or diagnostic logs. We do not operate a user account system on this website. For details about provider-side processing, consult GitHub's Privacy Statement.

3. Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is configured on this website to measure aggregate website usage, such as page views and general interaction patterns. The measurement ID is used to send analytics events to Google. Our on-site consent banner keeps analytics storage denied until a visitor chooses “Allow analytics”; visitors can instead choose “Reject optional analytics”. You can change your choice by clearing this website’s local storage for the domain and reloading the page. See Google’s privacy information for details about Google’s processing.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use technologies that are necessary for basic delivery, security or functionality. Third-party services such as externally hosted fonts may receive technical request information when your browser loads those resources. GA4 analytics storage is controlled by the consent banner described above and remains denied unless you opt in. We have not activated advertising cookies or Google AdSense on this website as of the effective date of this policy.

If we introduce advertising, embedded media or other non-essential technologies, we will update this notice and implement an appropriate consent mechanism where required. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

5. Advertising and third-party partners

Google AdSense and other advertising services are not currently represented as active on this website. If advertising is introduced, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise ads in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements. This policy will be updated before such services are activated.

For personalised ads served to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. See Google's publisher consent requirements.

6. How we use information

Information you send us may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss potential services, communicate about a project, maintain business records, protect the website and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Sharing and service providers

We may rely on service providers such as hosting, email and infrastructure providers to operate the website and respond to enquiries. They process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

8. Retention and security

We retain enquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, legitimate business administration and legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general business and professional audience and is not directed at children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the website.

11. International visitors

Our website may be accessed from countries other than the country in which its operator is based. Hosting and service providers may process technical information in locations where they or their subprocessors operate. Applicable privacy protections may vary by jurisdiction.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact narendrago999@gmail.com.

This notice is not legal advice. Please obtain appropriate legal review before enabling analytics, advertising or processing personal information at scale.
