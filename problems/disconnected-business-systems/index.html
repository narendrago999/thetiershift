01 / THE REAL-WORLD SCENARIO

What is actually going wrong?

When systems do not share reliable data, teams re-key information, reconcile conflicting records and make decisions from partial views. Point-to-point integrations can make the problem worse if they are not governed.

Symptoms teams commonly notice

  • Staff copy the same customer or order details between systems
  • Updates appear in one application but not another
  • Integrations fail without a clear replay path
  • Different systems use different identifiers and status meanings
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Look beyond the visible symptom.

The first visible failure is not always the root cause. Trace the full workflow, preserve run-level evidence and validate the business outcome before changing production behaviour.

    04 / SOLUTION DESIGN

    How to solve it reliably

    A production solution should address the root cause, make failures observable and support safe recovery.

    1. 01
      Map business entities, system-of-record ownership and the events that must move between applications.
    2. 02
      Use APIs or events according to the required latency and coupling; avoid unnecessary synchronous dependencies.
    3. 03
      Adopt stable correlation IDs, idempotency keys, bounded retries and dead-letter handling.
    4. 04
      Transform source payloads into versioned canonical contracts and monitor delivery lag and failures.
    5. 05
      Reconcile important business entities across systems and provide an operational replay process.

    07 / PREVENTION CHECKLIST

    Keep the problem from returning

    • Map ownership and flows: Identify system of record, entity keys, events and latency requirements.
    • Choose integration pattern: Use synchronous APIs for request/response and events for decoupled propagation.
    • Build resilient delivery: Add validation, idempotency, retry limits, dead letters and replay.
    • Operate and reconcile: Monitor end-to-end delivery and compare critical entities periodically.
