COMMON QUESTIONS

AI agents, explained.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an application that can use an AI model together with defined instructions, context and approved tools to carry out a task or sequence of tasks. The level of autonomy depends on the design and controls.

Can we hire an AI agent instead of building one?

Yes. TierShift can offer selected ready-to-deploy agents through an agreed subscription, usage-based or managed service arrangement. Availability and configuration depend on the use case and your operating environment.

Can you build an agent for our own process?

Yes. We can scope and develop a custom agent around your workflows, business rules, data sources and approved applications.

Will an AI agent have unrestricted access to our systems?

No. Access should be limited to the tools, records and actions required for its defined task. Sensitive or consequential actions can require human approval, and activities can be logged for review.

Can multiple agents work together?

Yes. A workflow can coordinate specialised agents, such as one that gathers information, another that validates it and a final agent that prepares an output. The process should include clear ownership, monitoring and escalation.

Do AI agents replace employees?

Our focus is to assist teams with repetitive, information-heavy or well-defined tasks. People remain essential for judgement, exceptions, accountability and decisions that require human context.
