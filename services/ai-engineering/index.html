COMMON QUESTIONS

AI engineering, explained.

What is AI engineering?

AI engineering is the practice of designing, building, integrating and operating applications that use AI models. It includes data preparation, model selection, orchestration, evaluation, security and deployment.

What is the difference between RAG and fine-tuning?

RAG retrieves relevant information from a knowledge source at query time and provides it to a model as context. Fine-tuning changes model behaviour through additional training. The right choice depends on the use case, data and expected outcomes.

Can AI agents work with our existing business systems?

They can be designed to interact with approved systems through APIs and other controlled tools. Access permissions, validation, auditability and human approval should be considered as part of the design.

Do we need to have our data platform ready?

Not necessarily. We can first assess the available data, its quality and access constraints, then recommend a practical starting point. Some projects may need data preparation before an AI solution can deliver reliable results.

How do you approach AI security and reliability?

Controls are selected for the application and its risk profile. Typical considerations include access control, data handling, grounded responses, evaluation, monitoring, human review and clear boundaries on what an AI system can do.
