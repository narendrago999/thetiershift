COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about data management & operations.

What is data management and operations?

It covers the practices and recurring processes used to keep business data accurate, consistent, governed and available for its intended use.

Can you help with data quality issues across multiple systems?

Yes. We can profile data across agreed sources, identify patterns and root causes, and define a prioritised remediation approach.

Do you provide ongoing operational support?

Support can be structured around agreed recurring checks, exception handling, reconciliations and data maintenance responsibilities.

Does this replace a data engineering platform?

No. Data management complements data engineering. It focuses on quality, stewardship and operational reliability, while engineering builds and operates the data movement and platform foundations.
