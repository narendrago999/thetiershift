COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about digital experience & web solutions.

How is this different from custom software development?

Digital experience work focuses on the public-facing website, user journeys, content, UX, SEO and performance. Custom software focuses more on application workflows, business logic and integrations.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can review the current site and prioritise design, content, technical SEO, accessibility and performance improvements.

Do you provide SEO services?

We provide technical SEO foundations such as metadata, crawlability, canonical URLs, structured data, internal linking and page performance. Search performance also depends on content quality and ongoing authority-building.

Can you manage hosting and deployment?

We can help configure domains, HTTPS, hosting and deployment workflows, with responsibilities agreed to fit your environment.
