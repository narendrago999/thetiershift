COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about it consulting & digital transformation.

What does IT consulting include?

It can include technology strategy, architecture, platform assessment, feasibility analysis, modernisation planning and delivery advisory.

Do you only provide recommendations, or can you help implement them?

We can support both advisory and engineering work. The engagement can move from assessment and roadmap into solution design and implementation where appropriate.

Can you assess our existing systems?

Yes. We can review agreed applications, data platforms, integrations, infrastructure and processes, subject to access and stakeholder availability.

Will the roadmap be tied to a specific vendor?

Recommendations should follow business requirements and constraints. We aim to explain trade-offs and avoid unnecessary technology choices or lock-in.
