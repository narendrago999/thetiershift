Your data pipeline can be running successfully — and still be delivering the wrong data.

A column changes type. A business definition drifts. A source arrives late. Downstream dashboards and AI workflows quietly inherit the problem.

Data contracts help teams agree what a data product promises before other teams depend on it.

A useful starting contract covers:
• Schema — fields, types and keys
• Business meaning — definitions, units and grain
• Quality and freshness — the checks that matter
• Ownership — who responds when something breaks
• Change management — how updates are versioned and communicated

You do not need to contract every table on day one. Start with one high-value dataset, automate its most important checks, and make ownership clear.

The aim is simple: fewer surprises downstream, and more trust in the data people use to make decisions.

How does your team manage changes to shared datasets today — formal contracts, automated tests, or mostly team-to-team communication?
