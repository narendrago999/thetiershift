WHAT TO NOTICE

Retries should be safe to repeat.

The same event may be delivered more than once. A stable key and deterministic ordering help the curated table converge on one valid record per business entity.

  • Stable business or event key
  • Deterministic latest-record rule
  • Idempotent upsert / MERGE
  • Reconciliation and replay evidence
Explore the full ingestion solution →
Production principle

Keep raw ingestion traceable, deduplicate with a documented key and ordering rule, then use transactional upsert semantics appropriate to the platform.
